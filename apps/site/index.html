From finding a manga to reading it

Getting manga onto an e-reader used to be a long chain of chores: find a site, save hundreds of pages, crop, resize, convert, copy. MangaPoint does all of it, in one app.

Find it

Type a title and every source you installed is asked at once. The same series from several sources becomes one card.

Get it

Pick chapters, or type a range like 1-20, 25, 30-. Downloads pick up where they stopped, and a failed page is tried on another source.

Read it

Your library keeps every series with its cover and your place, and Continue opens the reader where you stopped.

Send it

Pages are made for your reader's own screen, then go to a folder, a USB drive or SD card, or over Wi-Fi.

Every chapter, in one list

Open a series to see every chapter from all its sources merged into one list, with the sources that have each one and any gaps flagged. Filter it, sort it, or type a range, and add them to the downloads.

  • Many sources searched at the same time
  • A queue with pause, resume, retry and reordering
  • Picks up where it stopped if you close the app

Read it right there

Downloaded and imported series open in MangaPoint's own reader: a chapter is one long strip at a width that suits the window, with the chapter picker at the top, Previous and Next at the end, and zoom that keeps your place.

  • Your place is kept for every series
  • Bring in your own CBZ, ZIP, CBR, RAR, 7Z, EPUB and PDF books, folders and pictures
  • Drop files on the Library to import them

Made for your reader's screen

Pages are made at your reader's exact size and density, so nothing is scaled again on the device. A live preview shows the page as downloaded next to the page made for your reader.

  • Automatic or manual cropping, spreads and long strips cut into screens
  • Contrast, dithering, landscape, zoom and guide arrows
  • Presets for manga, manhwa and manhua, or your own

Works with your reader

Built for the Xteink readers, with standard EPUB files for Kindle, Kobo, Boox, PocketBook and reMarkable, and CBZ for the readers that open it. A catalog of 29 readers comes with their screens and formats, and you can add your own by its size.

  • Xteink X4 · X4 Pro · X3
  • Kindle
  • Kobo
  • Boox
  • PocketBook
  • reMarkable
  • XTC black and white
  • XTCH four grays
  • EPUB fixed layout
  • CBZ with ComicInfo
  • Color for color screens

Read on any device

The MangaPoint website reads and exports the books you already have, in any browser, on a computer, a tablet or a phone. It works like the app: the same Library, reader and Export screen. It does not search or download manga; the desktop app does.

  • Read offlineInstall it and it opens with no connection at all.
  • From your home screenIt opens like an app, without the browser around it.
  • Kept on this deviceYour books are never uploaded.
