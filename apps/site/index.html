What is MangaPoint?

MangaPoint is a free app for Windows, macOS and Linux that gets manga onto your e-reader. Type a title, tick the chapters you want, and it downloads them, makes files your reader opens at the exact size of its screen, and sends them over.

It has its own reader too, so you can read on your computer, and a website that reads the books you already have on any phone or tablet.

It was made for the Xteink readers (X4, X4 Pro, X3) and also makes EPUB files for Kindle, Kobo, Boox, PocketBook and reMarkable, and CBZ comic archives for the readers that open them.

How it works

From a title to a book on your reader, in one app. This is the whole way through, with the screens you will see.

Step 1

Search every source at once

Type a title and every source you installed is asked at the same time. The same series from several sources becomes one card, with its cover, how many chapters it has and when it was last updated. You choose your sources once, when you first start the app.

Step 2

Pick the chapters you want

A series page shows its details and every chapter from all its sources in one list, with the sources that have each one and any gaps flagged. Tick chapters, filter and sort them, or type a range like 1-20, 25, 30-.

Step 3

Download, and let it run

Downloads go one series after another in Tasks, with pause, resume, retry and reordering. If a page fails, another source is tried. Close the app and it picks up where it stopped; a notification tells you when something is done.

Step 4

Keep a library, and read it there

What you download is in your Library, with how much is downloaded and exported and where you stopped. Continue opens MangaPoint's own reader on that page: each chapter is one long strip, with the chapter picker at the top and zoom that keeps your place.

  • Bring in books you already have: CBZ, ZIP, CBR, RAR, 7Z, EPUB and PDF, folders and pictures
  • Drop them on the Library, or press Import
Step 5

Made for your reader's screen

Tick a series and press Export. Pages are made at your reader's exact size and density, so nothing is scaled again on the device, and the preview shows the page as downloaded next to the page your reader will get.

  • Automatic or manual cropping, double-page spreads, long strips cut into screens
  • Contrast, dithering, landscape, zoom and guide arrows
  • Start from a preset for manga, manhwa or manhua, or keep your own
Step 6

Send it to your reader

Choose your reader and where the files go: a folder, a USB drive or SD card (found for you, with a "Safe to unplug" message when it is done), or Wi-Fi. Then pick the format, and one file per series, per chapter or every few chapters.

  • XTC black and white
  • XTCH four grays
  • EPUB for most readers
  • CBZ comic archive
  • Color for color screens
Once

Your reader, set up once

A catalog of 29 readers comes with their screen sizes, densities and formats; yours not there? Add it by its name and size. Each reader keeps its own folder, way of sending, firmware and formats, and the Export screen only offers what your reader opens.

On the web

Read on any device

The MangaPoint website reads and exports the books you already have, in any browser, on a computer, a tablet or a phone. It works like the app, with the same Library, reader and Export screen. It does not search or download manga; the desktop app does.

  • Read offlineInstall it and it opens with no connection at all.
  • From your home screenIt opens like an app, without the browser around it.
  • Kept on this deviceYour books are never uploaded.

No account. No server of ours.

Everything runs on your computer, or in your browser on the web. There is nothing to sign up for, and nothing you read is sent anywhere. MangaPoint is free and open source under the MIT license.
